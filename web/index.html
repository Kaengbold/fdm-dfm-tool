            How it's printed:

            The outside of a print is solid: walls around the sides, solid layers on top and bottom. Inside is a light infill grid. Most strength comes from the solid outside. These go into the 3MF too.

            Detailed check from your sliced print

            The most exact check. Export the 3MF (below) in this orientation, slice it in OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio or PrusaSlicer, save the G-code and load it here. The check then uses the walls, infill and line directions your printer will really print. A printed line is strong along itself and weaker across, like the layers.
